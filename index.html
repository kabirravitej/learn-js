Ideas you will meet

These words show up a lot in JavaScript. You do not need to memorize them — you will learn them by using them.

Variables

Named boxes that hold values you can reuse and update.

let score = 0;

Strings

Text wrapped in quotes — names, messages, anything you say.

"Hello, Milo"

Numbers

Math for scores, prices, timers, and pixel positions.

price * 1.1

Booleans

True or false — the yes/no answers that power decisions.

isReady === true

Arrays

Ordered lists: todos, colors, high scores, player names.

["a", "b", "c"]

Objects

Group related details together under one name.

{ name: "Milo" }

Beginner tips from Milo

Small habits that make learning JavaScript much smoother.

  • Type, don’t only read. Your fingers learn patterns your eyes skip past.
  • Break things on purpose. Errors are clues, not failures — the console is your friend.
  • Keep sessions short. Fifteen focused minutes beats two confused hours.
  • Name things clearly. playerScore beats x every time.
  • Celebrate tiny wins. First console.log counts. First button click counts more.
  • Come back tomorrow. Sleep is part of learning — ideas settle overnight.
